Maya’s fiveDani’s 40th

Five frames that close into one square

Four frames turn around a fifth. Two are landscape and two are portrait, the way guests actually hold their phones. The fifth is square and sits in the middle. Together they tile one square with nothing left over. The tagline is the construction: every guest’s frames are separate pieces, and together they make one picture.

How I got here. I worked through four other routes and dropped each one. A five-frame film strip was the brief’s “five boxes in a row”. An F monogram built from viewfinder corners read as a generic initial and fell apart at 16px. Stacked prints turned into a blob when small. A squared 5 drawn by one continuous five-frame strip read as an S on a digital clock. The square survived reduction: one silhouette, solid at every size, and it looks right before anyone explains it.

The details that make it ownable. No four corners ever meet (the same rule as a tatami floor), so no seam cuts across the mark and it reads as one turning group, not a grid. The gutter is a fifth of a unit, and the corner radius matches the product’s printed photos. Colour lives in the centre: violet on white, highlight violet on ink, white on violet. The wordmark repeats that centre as the dot on the i.

The wordmark is Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold, the weight the app already uses, converted to outlines, kerned and tracked 1.4% tighter. Its one custom change is a square dot on the i. It stays in the product’s type family so it sits beside Fraunces and Jakarta without competing. Its source is still a public typeface, so a type designer should redraw the letterforms before any trademark filing.

Ownability tests

  • Without the name PassA pinwheel square with a violet heart. One shape, easy to recall and redraw from memory.
  • 16px PassA hand-drawn favicon on the pixel grid: 4px frames, 1px gutters. A dark-tab version swaps to white.
  • One colour PassEvery version is solid shapes, with no gradients, outlines or tones.
  • Beside a QR PassIts large flat planes contrast with the QR’s fine module texture, so it stays distinct at table-card size.
  • On a photograph PassWhite, single colour, placed in a corner. It never sits over faces.
  • Birthday, wedding, offsite PassNo hearts, confetti or cameras, and nothing tied to one kind of occasion.
  • Trademark clearance OpenPinwheel tilings are an old pattern, and layout apps use similar tiles. Ownership comes from these exact proportions, the violet centre and consistent use. It still needs a formal search.
  • Photography OpenPanel 08 is a drawn light study. The project’s rules rule out stock images, so real event photography comes later.
